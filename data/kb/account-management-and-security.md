# VoltKart Account Management and Security

Managing your VoltKart account is easy and secure, providing centralized access to your order history, delivery addresses, warranties, and wallet credits.

## Account Creation and Profile Updates

Setting up and maintaining your profile ensures a seamless electronics shopping experience:
- **Registration**: You can create an account using a valid 10-digit Indian mobile number or email address. A One-Time Password (OTP) verification is required to complete signup.
- **Address Book**: Save multiple delivery addresses (Home, Office, Other) with complete pincodes, landmarks, and contact numbers. Accurate pincodes ensure accurate shipping rates (such as free delivery on orders of Rs 999 or above, or the Rs 49 fee for orders below Rs 999).
- **Editing Information**: Update your primary email, phone number, and billing details under "Account Settings" > "Profile". Changing your registered mobile number requires OTP verification on both old and new numbers.
- **GST Information**: Save your 15-digit GSTIN under business details to streamline automatic B2B tax invoice generation for all future purchases.

## Login Security, OTP, and Two-Factor Authentication

Protecting your personal and financial credentials is our priority:
- **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**: We recommend enabling SMS or authenticator-based 2FA under "Security Settings" for added protection on login and during payment authorization.
- **Suspicious Activity**: If our fraud detection systems detect an unusual login from an unverified location or device, your session will be locked, and a verification email will be dispatched immediately.
- **Password Reset**: If you forget your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login screen. A secure password reset link will be sent to your registered email, valid for 15 minutes.
- **Never Share OTPs**: VoltKart customer care executives or delivery personnel will never ask for your account password, UPI PIN, or bank CVV.

## Account Deactivation and Data Privacy

You retain complete control over your personal data under Indian Information Technology laws and VoltKart privacy policy:
- **Temporary Deactivation**: You can pause your account temporarily under "Account Settings". Active orders, pending refunds (scheduled within the standard 5-7 business days), or ongoing 30-day return pickups will continue to process normally.
- **Permanent Account Deletion**: Submit a data erasure request through customer support. Your profile, stored addresses, and marketing preferences will be deleted within 30 days, while statutory tax invoices and warranty records are archived as required by law.
- **Store Credit Warning**: Any unexpired VoltKart Store Credit or active gift cards must be redeemed prior to permanent account deletion, as remaining balances cannot be refunded in cash once an account is erased.
