# Reporting Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Deliveries

At VoltKart, we take product condition and accuracy seriously. If an order arrives damaged in transit, fails to power on, or contains the wrong model, we provide expedited resolution.

## 48-Hour Reporting Window and Required Evidence

To expedite a claim for damaged, defective, or incorrect shipments, issues must be reported within 48 hours of physical package delivery:
- **Intact Packaging Warning**: If the outer shipping carton shows severe crushing, punctures, or broken security tape upon delivery, note this with the courier or refuse delivery.
- **Reporting Channels**: Contact VoltKart customer support within 48 hours via "My Orders", email at support@voltkart.in, or by calling our toll-free number 1800-890-VOLT.
- **Required Documentation**:
  - High-resolution photos or a continuous unboxing video showing the outer package with the shipping label visible.
  - Clear images of the device, visible damage, or the incorrect serial number/model label.
  - A copy of the tax invoice included inside the parcel.

Failure to report transit damage or missing accessories within 48 hours may limit eligibility to standard manufacturer warranty coverage rather than an immediate doorstep replacement.

## Priority Replacement and Inspection Protocol

Once your claim is submitted and logged in our system:
1. Our technical validation team reviews the uploaded images or unboxing video within 24 hours.
2. An expedited reverse pickup is scheduled with our priority courier partner at no cost to you.
3. Upon pickup or initial photographic verification, an identical replacement unit is expedited from our warehouse with priority courier delivery (taking 2 to 3 business days in metro areas and 3 to 5 business days in other regions).
4. All replacement devices carry a fresh 30-day return window and the full 1-year brand warranty.

## Full Refund Resolution Options

If a replacement unit is unavailable, or if you prefer a complete refund:
- VoltKart will process a 100% refund, including any original shipping charges or cash-on-delivery handling fees.
- Refunds to digital modes (UPI, Debit/Credit Card, Net Banking) are completed within 5-7 business days from receipt of the damaged item.
- Alternatively, customers may opt for instant VoltKart Store Credit, credited to their account wallet within 2 hours of verification and valid for 1 year (365 days).
