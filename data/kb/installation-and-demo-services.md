# Electronics Installation and Product Demo Services

VoltKart coordinates professional installation and demonstration services for major electronics, televisions, audio systems, and home smart appliances.

## Eligible Categories and Scheduling Windows

For complex consumer electronics, VoltKart coordinates seamless doorstep setup:
- **Covered Products**: Smart televisions (43 inches and larger), home theater soundbar systems, wall-mounted monitors, smart home security setups, and heavy desktop workstations.
- **Automated Scheduling**: When your order is placed, an installation ticket is automatically created with the brand's authorized service network.
- **Service Timelines**: In metro cities, technicians arrive within 24 to 48 hours of product delivery. In non-metro locations, service is completed within 2 to 4 business days post-delivery.
- **Slot Confirmation**: You will receive an SMS and phone call from the authorized technician to confirm a convenient 2-hour appointment slot (available 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Monday through Saturday).

## Authorized Brand Technicians and Service Inclusions

All installation tasks are executed by certified manufacturer service personnel:
- **Standard Installation Inclusions**: Unboxing the unit, mounting on standard wall brackets or placing on a tabletop, electrical connectivity setup, initial device boot-up, Wi-Fi pairing, and a basic operational demo.
- **Standard Accessories**: Basic wall-mount kits provided inside the box are installed at zero additional cost.
- **Additional Accessories**: Specialized swivel brackets, extension power cables, or specialized conduit piping are payable directly to the technician against an official company rate-card receipt.
- **Inspection Checklist**: The technician will inspect the panel for physical defects or dead pixels and endorse your warranty sheet.

## Unboxing Precautions and Warranty Safeguards

To prevent accidental damage disputes and safeguard your warranty rights:
- **Do Not Self-Unbox**: For large televisions and delicate home audio setups marked with "Open Box by Brand Only", please do not break the seal or unpack the unit prior to the technician's arrival.
- **Transit Damage Reporting**: If the technician discovers transit damage or internal screen cracking upon opening the box, they will file an instant Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) inspection certificate.
- **Expedited Replacement**: With a technician's DOA certificate, VoltKart will schedule an expedited priority replacement or process a 100% refund within 5-7 business days.
- All installed electronics remain fully covered under VoltKart's 30-day return window and the 1-year manufacturer warranty.
