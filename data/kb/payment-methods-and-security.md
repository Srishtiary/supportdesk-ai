# Accepted Payment Methods and Payment Security

VoltKart offers an array of secure payment channels to ensure smooth and trustworthy online transactions for electronics shoppers across India.

## Supported Online Payment Options

VoltKart supports numerous reliable payment channels with zero transaction surcharges:
- **Credit and Debit Cards**: Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Maestro, and American Express cards issued by all major Indian and international banks.
- **Unified Payments Interface (UPI)**: Instant UPI payments via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and bank-specific UPI apps.
- **Internet Banking**: Direct net banking access across more than 55 leading commercial and cooperative banks in India.
- **Digital Wallets**: Selected digital wallets such as Paytm Wallet, Mobikwik, and Amazon Pay balance.
- **VoltKart Store Credit**: Instant redemption of store credits or gift cards valid for 1 year from issuance.

All digital payments qualify for promotional bank discount codes and instant cashback offers where available.

## Cash on Delivery (COD) Guidelines

For customers who prefer paying at their doorstep upon physical receipt of the package:
- **Order Value Limits**: Cash on Delivery is available for order totals between Rs 299 and Rs 10,000 across serviceable postal codes.
- **Handling Charges**: COD orders above Rs 2,000 enjoy free cash handling. Orders below Rs 2,000 incur a standard convenience fee of Rs 40.
- **Payment at Doorstep**: Courier personnel accept exact cash, and many logistics executives now carry QR code scanners allowing instant UPI payment upon delivery.
- **Refunds for COD**: Returned or cancelled COD merchandise is refunded directly to your bank account via NEFT/IMPS within 5-7 business days or credited instantly as VoltKart store credit.

## Transaction Security and Data Protection

VoltKart enforces rigorous security protocols to protect all financial information:
- All web and mobile sessions are encrypted using industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption.
- We strictly comply with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) tokenization guidelines and PCI-DSS Level 1 standards; we never store your complete CVV or card PIN.
- Online payments require mandatory Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) via OTP or biometric approval sent by your issuing bank.
- In the event of a payment deduction where order placement fails, our automated gateway reconciles the charge and reverses the funds within 5-7 business days.
