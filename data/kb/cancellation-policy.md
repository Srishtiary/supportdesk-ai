# VoltKart Order Cancellation Guidelines

VoltKart allows customers the flexibility to cancel orders easily when shopping plans change.

## Cancellation Prior to Dispatch

Orders can be cancelled free of charge at any moment prior to dispatch from our warehouse:
- To cancel an unfulfilled order, navigate to "My Orders" in your VoltKart account, click on the specific order, and choose "Cancel Order".
- Select a cancellation reason from the options provided and confirm the cancellation.
- Once cancelled, an immediate confirmation notification is dispatched via SMS and registered email.
- Prepaid orders cancelled prior to dispatch receive an automatic 100% refund initiated immediately.

If an order contains multiple items, you may cancel individual products without affecting the remaining items in your order, provided the remaining cart value still meets any promotion terms.

## Cancellation After Dispatch or at Delivery

Once an order has been packed and handed over to our logistics partner, it cannot be cancelled via the website or mobile app interface:
- **Refusing Doorstep Delivery**: If your order has already shipped, you can simply refuse delivery when the courier partner arrives and requests your confirmation OTP. Inform the delivery agent that you wish to reject the package.
- **RTO (Return to Origin)**: Once the rejected parcel is tagged as RTO and returns to our fulfillment center, our team will verify package integrity.
- **Post-Delivery Return**: If you or an authorized representative accepted the delivery by providing the OTP, you can still return the item under our standard 30-day return policy.

## Refund Processing for Cancelled Orders

Refund timelines for cancelled items depend on the payment method and cancellation stage:
- **Credit Card / Debit Card / Net Banking**: Refund is initiated immediately upon cancellation and reflects in your bank statement within 5-7 business days.
- **UPI Payments**: UPI refunds are processed within 24 to 48 hours and typically credit back within 2-3 business days.
- **VoltKart Store Credit**: Instant credit is added to your account balance immediately upon cancellation confirmation and is valid for 1 year (365 days).
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: For COD orders cancelled before or during delivery, no charge is levied and no refund processing is required.
