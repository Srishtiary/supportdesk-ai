# VoltKart Refund Policy and Timelines

VoltKart is committed to providing prompt and transparent refunds whenever an order is cancelled or a returned product is verified at our testing facility.

## Refund Processing Timelines

Once an order cancellation is approved or a returned product passes quality check at our fulfillment center, refunds are processed according to the following schedules:
- **Card and Digital Payments**: Refunds to Credit Cards, Debit Cards, and Net Banking accounts are initiated within 24 hours of inspection approval and reflect in your account within 5-7 business days, depending on your card issuer or banking partner.
- **Unified Payments Interface (UPI)**: UPI refunds (via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM) are processed within 24 to 48 hours and typically credit to your linked bank account within 2-3 business days.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: For orders delivered via Cash on Delivery, refunds are transferred via National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT) or Immediate Payment Service (IMPS) within 5-7 business days after customer bank account details are verified.
- **VoltKart Store Credit**: Instant credit is added to your VoltKart wallet immediately upon inspection approval.

All refunds are subject to our standard 30-day return evaluation window.

## Payment Methods and Refund Destination

Refunds are credited strictly to the source account utilized during purchase:
- **Prepaid Cards and Accounts**: All prepaid transactions (Credit/Debit/Net Banking) cannot be redirected to a third-party account or different card for security and anti-fraud compliance.
- **COD Verification Process**: If you paid via Cash on Delivery, our support portal will request your bank account holder name, account number, and IFSC code. The account holder name must match the name registered on your VoltKart profile.
- **EMI Orders**: For orders purchased with credit card EMI, the full amount will be credited back to your credit card within 5-7 business days. You will need to contact your card issuer to cancel the ongoing EMI schedule or adjust processing fees.

Shipping charges are fully refunded if the entire order is cancelled before dispatch or returned due to manufacturing defects or shipping damage.

## VoltKart Store Credit and Instant Settlements

Customers who prefer immediate resolution can opt for VoltKart Store Credit instead of waiting for banking channels:
- Store credits are credited to your VoltKart account wallet instantly upon return approval.
- Store credits carry a validity of 1 year (365 days) from the date of issue.
- Store credits can be applied in full or partial amounts towards any product or brand on the VoltKart platform.
- Unused store credits cannot be exchanged for cash or withdrawn directly to bank accounts.
