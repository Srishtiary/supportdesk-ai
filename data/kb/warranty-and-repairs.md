# VoltKart Manufacturer Warranty and Repair Guidelines

Every brand-new electronics item sold on VoltKart comes backed by official manufacturer warranty coverage, ensuring peace of mind throughout your ownership.

## Standard 1-Year Warranty Protection

All brand-new electronic devices—including laptops, smartphones, tablets, PC components, televisions, monitors, and audio gear—include a standard 1-year brand warranty from the date of delivery:
- Refurbished or open-box clearance electronics (when explicitly labeled) include a 6-month VoltKart certified functional warranty.
- Warranty coverage is validated directly through the original VoltKart GST tax invoice.
- Serial numbers on the product chassis and packaging must match the serial number recorded in your VoltKart order history.
- The standard 1-year warranty is completely independent of our 30-day return policy and covers technical performance beyond the initial 30 days.

## Inclusions and Exclusions

Understanding the scope of warranty coverage helps ensure quick service resolution:
- **What Is Covered**: Manufacturing defects, internal motherboard or PCB failure, power supply failure under normal operation, display panel pixel defects exceeding manufacturer threshold limits, and audio driver malfunction.
- **What Is Excluded**: Physical breakage, drops, dents, cracked display panels, liquid spill damage, burn marks from external voltage surges, damages caused by unauthorized repair or third-party disassembly, and natural battery capacity degradation after prolonged normal use.
- Products missing their original serial number or IMEI label cannot be serviced under warranty.

## Warranty Claim and Service Center Support

To avail of repair or replacement services under the standard 1-year warranty:
1. **Locate Invoice**: Download your GST invoice from "My Orders" on the VoltKart portal or app.
2. **Authorized Service Centers**: Visit the nearest authorized service center of the respective brand (such as Asus, Samsung, HP, Lenovo, Dell, Sony, or Apple) with the product, original bill, and product packaging.
3. **VoltKart Assistance**: If an authorized brand service center is unavailable within 50 km of your location, contact VoltKart Customer Care at 1800-890-VOLT (open 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM IST, Monday through Sunday) or support@voltkart.in. We will facilitate factory repairs or help coordinate pickup with the brand service team.
4. **Resolution Timeline**: Most service repairs take between 7 to 14 business days depending on spare part availability.
