# VoltKart Gift Cards and Store Credit Guidelines

VoltKart digital gift cards and store credits offer a simple, flexible way to purchase electronic gadgets and accessories.

## Purchasing, Gifting, and Denominations

VoltKart electronic gift cards make the perfect present for technology enthusiasts:
- **Denominations**: Digital gift cards are available in preset amounts of Rs 500, Rs 1,000, Rs 2,500, Rs 5,000, and Rs 10,000, or customizable amounts up to Rs 50,000.
- **Instant Delivery**: Gift cards are delivered via email or SMS within 15 minutes of payment confirmation, complete with a 16-digit card code and a secure 6-digit PIN.
- **Personalized Messages**: You can include a custom greeting message and specify a future delivery date for birthdays or anniversaries.
- **Payment Modes**: Gift cards can be purchased using credit/debit cards, net banking, or UPI. Gift cards cannot be purchased using Cash on Delivery (COD) or another gift card.

## Redemption Rules and Validity Periods

Redeeming store credits and gift cards is seamless across our entire catalog:
- **Redemption at Checkout**: Enter the 16-digit voucher number and PIN under the "Gift Cards & Credits" field on the checkout screen.
- **Partial Use**: If your order total is less than your gift card or store credit balance, the remaining funds remain securely in your account for future purchases.
- **Split Payments**: If your order exceeds the credit balance, you can pay the remainder using any other accepted payment method (such as UPI, credit card, net banking, or COD if eligible).
- **Free Shipping Threshold**: If the final payable value of your electronics order is Rs 999 or higher (regardless of gift card contribution), you receive free standard shipping. Orders under Rs 999 are charged Rs 49.
- **Validity**: Both VoltKart Gift Cards and Store Credits remain valid for exactly 1 year (365 days) from the date of issue. Unused balances expire automatically after 365 days.

## Balances, Security, and Return Credits

Key terms governing credit balances and refunds:
- **Non-Transferable**: Store credits and gift cards cannot be transferred to another user's account, exchanged for cash, or withdrawn to a bank account.
- **Refunds from Purchases**: If you return an item originally paid for with store credit under our 30-day return policy, the refunded balance is credited back into your store credit wallet immediately upon return approval.
- **Protecting Your PIN**: Treat your gift card code and PIN with the same confidentiality as cash. VoltKart cannot replace lost, stolen, or unauthorized gift card codes.
