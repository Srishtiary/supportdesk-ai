# Shipping Rates, Options, and Delivery Timelines

VoltKart delivers electronics, computing gear, home audio, and accessories across more than 19,000 postal codes across India.

## Shipping Charges and Free Delivery Threshold

VoltKart offers competitive and transparent delivery charges for all orders:
- **Free Standard Shipping**: All orders with a total cart value of Rs 999 or above qualify for 100% free standard delivery anywhere in India.
- **Standard Shipping Fee**: For orders with a cart value below Rs 999, a nominal flat shipping fee of Rs 49 is applied at checkout.
- **Express Shipping**: Available on eligible pin codes in Tier 1 cities for a flat fee of Rs 149, regardless of the order total.
- **Cash on Delivery Charges**: COD orders of Rs 2,000 and above qualify for free COD handling, while orders below Rs 2,000 carry a nominal handling fee of Rs 40. COD is capped at a maximum order value of Rs 10,000 (with a minimum cart value of Rs 299).

Shipping fees are clearly itemized on your final checkout screen before you confirm payment.

## Delivery Timelines Across India

Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on business days are dispatched from our nearest regional fulfillment center on the same day:
- **Metro Cities (Standard)**: Delivery within 2 to 3 business days across Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, and Pune.
- **Tier 2 and Tier 3 Cities (Standard)**: Delivery within 3 to 5 business days across state capitals and regional hubs.
- **Remote and North-East Regions**: Delivery within 5 to 7 business days depending on road and air transit connectivity.
- **Express Delivery**: Delivered within 1 to 2 business days in select metro pin codes.

Deliveries are scheduled between 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM IST from Monday to Saturday, excluding national holidays.

## Packaging Standards and Courier Partners

We ensure your delicate electronic equipment reaches you in pristine condition:
- All sensitive products (smartphones, monitors, graphics cards, motherboards, laptops) are shipped with multi-layered shockproof bubble wrap, tamper-evident tape, and rigid corrugated outer boxes.
- Our primary national courier partners include Blue Dart, Delhivery, Shadowfax, and Xpressbees.
- Real-time tracking IDs and courier tracking URLs are sent via SMS and registered email immediately upon package handover.
- Open-box delivery verification is available in select metro postal codes to inspect external physical condition before sharing your delivery OTP.
