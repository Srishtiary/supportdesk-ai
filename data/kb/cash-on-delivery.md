# Cash on Delivery (COD) Guidelines and Terms

VoltKart provides Cash on Delivery (COD) for customers across India who prefer paying in cash upon arrival of their package.

## Order Thresholds and Convenience Fees

Cash on Delivery orders are subject to the following rules and service criteria:
- **Eligible Cart Value**: COD is applicable on orders with a minimum cart total of Rs 299 and a maximum cart total of Rs 10,000. Orders exceeding Rs 10,000 must be prepaid using credit/debit cards, UPI, or net banking.
- **Convenience Fee Structure**:
  - Orders of Rs 2,000 and above qualify for completely free COD processing.
  - Orders below Rs 2,000 incur a standard COD convenience fee of Rs 40.
- **Shipping Thresholds Apply**: If your overall order value is below Rs 999, the standard shipping charge of Rs 49 is added alongside the COD fee. Orders of Rs 999 or above receive free standard shipping.
- **Pincode Verification**: COD eligibility depends on courier partner coverage; some remote or restricted postal codes may require prepaid checkout.

## Delivery Acceptance and Handover Procedures

To ensure smooth receipt of your COD parcel:
- **Cash Preparation**: Please keep the exact cash amount ready prior to arrival of the courier agent, as delivery personnel do not always carry adequate change.
- **Digital Cash on Delivery**: Many of our delivery agents carry dynamic UPI QR codes. You can scan the agent's code using PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, or your banking app to complete payment on arrival.
- **No Open-Box Before Payment**: Unless you have specifically purchased an eligible open-box delivery item, couriers are required to collect payment and verify the delivery OTP before handing over the sealed package.
- If the recipient is unavailable, the courier will make up to three delivery attempts over three consecutive business days before returning the shipment to our fulfillment hub.

## Returns and Bank Refunds for COD Orders

Returning a COD order is straightforward and fully covered under our customer satisfaction policy:
- COD items enjoy the standard 30-day return policy and 1-year manufacturer warranty.
- When filing a return request, navigate to "My Orders" and input your bank account details (Account Number, Account Holder Name, and Bank IFSC).
- Once the returned device is picked up, inspected, and approved, your refund is credited via NEFT or IMPS within 5-7 business days directly to your verified bank account.
- Alternatively, you can select instant VoltKart Store Credit, which reflects immediately in your wallet and remains valid for 1 year (365 days).
