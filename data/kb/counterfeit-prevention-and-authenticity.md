# Product Authenticity and Anti-Counterfeit Policy

VoltKart takes an uncompromising stance on consumer safety and product genuineness, guaranteeing that every electronics product sold across our platform is 100% authentic.

## 100% Genuine Brand Assurance

When purchasing electronics on VoltKart, customers are protected by our strict authenticity guarantee:
- **Direct Brand Sourcing**: VoltKart procures merchandise directly from official brand manufacturers (such as Apple, Sony, Samsung, Dell, Asus, HP, Bose, and OnePlus) or their legally authorized national distributors in India.
- **Brand Packaging & Seals**: Products are shipped with original factory tamper-evident seals intact, inside manufacturer retail packaging with all original accessories.
- **Factory Warranty Valid**: Every new product is covered by the genuine standard 1-year brand warranty, recognized by all authorized brand service centers across India.
- **No Gray Market Goods**: We do not stock or facilitate parallel imports, unverified gray-market goods, refurbished items disguised as new, or cloned third-party imitations.

## Serial Number Tracking and Supply Chain Integrity

We deploy stringent supply chain monitoring to maintain end-to-end traceability:
- **Barcode & IMEI Registration**: When an order is processed at our fulfillment warehouse, the product's unique IMEI, MAC address, or serial number is scanned and recorded directly onto your GST tax invoice.
- **Batch Verification**: Quality control teams randomly inspect inward logistics batches to verify manufacturer holograms, BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) registration marks, and statutory labeling compliance.
- **Courier Tamper Seals**: Shipments are secured with tamper-evident branded security tape. Customers are instructed to inspect package seals before providing the delivery OTP.
- **Free Shipping Quality Guarantee**: All authentic shipments qualifying for free shipping above Rs 999 (or Rs 49 for smaller carts) receive the same rigorous inspection standards.

## Customer Verification and Reporting Protocol

If you ever have doubts regarding the authenticity of a product received:
- **Manufacturer Registration**: You can verify your item by registering its unique serial number or IMEI directly on the manufacturer's official web portal.
- **Authenticity Complaints**: If any item purchased on VoltKart is confirmed as counterfeit by an authorized brand service center, notify us within our 30-day return window.
- **Zero-Tolerance Resolution**: Upon verification, VoltKart will issue an immediate 100% refund within 5-7 business days, including all original delivery and handling charges, and initiate legal proceedings against the supplying source.
- Contact our customer grievance cell directly at support@voltkart.in or call 1800-890-VOLT for immediate support on authenticity verifications.
