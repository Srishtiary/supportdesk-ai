# EMI and No-Cost EMI Payment Options

VoltKart offers flexible Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) options, empowering customers to buy high-end electronics, laptops, and appliances with manageable monthly budgets.

## No-Cost EMI Eligibility and Terms

No-Cost EMI allows you to buy products without paying any additional interest:
- **Cart Minimum**: Available on orders with an aggregate cart value of Rs 3,000 or higher on eligible electronics and computing gear.
- **Tenure Options**: 3-month and 6-month repayment tenures qualify for No-Cost EMI on selected products.
- **How It Operates**: The bank interest calculated on your transaction is provided as an upfront instant discount at checkout by VoltKart, ensuring the total amount paid matches the original product price.
- **Partner Banks**: Available across leading credit cards issued by HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, SBI Card, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank, and standard card networks.

Please note that issuing banks may levy an upfront one-time processing fee and applicable GST per transaction according to their internal schedule of charges.

## Standard Low-Cost EMI Tenures and Partner Banks

For larger purchases or longer repayment timelines, standard EMI plans provide extended flexibility:
- **Tenure Duration**: Available for 9, 12, 18, and 24-month tenures on credit cards and selected pre-approved debit cards.
- **Interest Rates**: Standard interest rates range from 12% to 16% per annum, calculated on a reducing balance basis by your credit card issuer.
- **Cardless & Debit Card EMI**: Eligible customers with pre-approved banking profiles from HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Federal Bank can utilize Debit Card EMI or cardless finance without blocking a credit limit.
- **Credit Limit Block**: When placing an EMI order, your card issuer temporarily blocks the full invoice amount from your available credit limit, releasing it progressively with each monthly repayment.

## Cancellation, Returns, and Preclosure Terms

EMI purchases remain fully protected under VoltKart's consumer policies:
- If an EMI order is cancelled prior to shipping or returned under our 30-day return policy, the entire purchase price is refunded back to your credit card within 5-7 business days.
- Once the refund arrives at your bank, contact your credit card customer support to cancel the underlying installment arrangement and reverse future interest bookings.
- If you wish to preclose an active EMI plan early, you may do so directly with your bank, though standard banking preclosure charges may apply.
