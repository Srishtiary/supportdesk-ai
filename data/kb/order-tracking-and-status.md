# Tracking Your Order and Delivery Status

VoltKart provides end-to-end shipment visibility so you can monitor your electronics purchase from our fulfillment center directly to your doorstep.

## Real-Time Tracking and Dispatch Updates

Once your order is confirmed, tracking information is updated in real time:
- **Order Confirmation**: An instant confirmation email and SMS containing your VoltKart Order ID (e.g., VK-984210) are sent upon payment approval or COD confirmation.
- **Dispatch Notification**: Within 24 hours, once your parcel is packed and handed over to our delivery partner (Blue Dart, Delhivery, Shadowfax, or Xpressbees), you will receive a tracking link with an Air Waybill (AWB) number.
- **Live Tracking**: Access "My Orders" on the VoltKart portal or click the tracking link in your SMS to view live vehicle checkpoint milestones.
- **Out for Delivery**: When the shipment reaches your local delivery hub and is assigned to a courier agent, you will receive an SMS containing the delivery executive's contact number and a secure 4-digit Delivery OTP.

## Order Status Definitions and Milestones

Here is what each status milestone in your dashboard indicates:
- **Placed**: Your order has been registered, payment verified, and the fulfillment warehouse has received the packing order.
- **Processing**: The items are being picked, serial numbers scanned, anti-tamper security seals attached, and prepared for dispatch.
- **Shipped / In Transit**: The courier partner has collected the package and it is moving across the transit network to your destination city. Standard shipping arrives within 2 to 3 business days for metros and 3 to 5 business days for regional areas.
- **Out for Delivery**: The parcel is out with the local driver for delivery between 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM IST today.
- **Delivered**: The delivery OTP has been verified, handing custody of the package to you. Your 30-day return window and 1-year brand warranty begin from this day.

## Handling Delivery Delays and Exceptions

If your parcel encounters delays or delivery exceptions:
- **Severe Weather / Transit Restrictions**: In case of transport disruptions, the estimated delivery date on your tracking page will automatically update.
- **Failed Delivery Attempts**: If you are unavailable, the courier will make up to three attempts on consecutive business days before redirecting the parcel to our hub.
- **Missing or Stuck Packages**: If your tracking status remains unchanged for more than 48 hours without delivery, reach out to VoltKart Customer Care at 1800-890-VOLT or support@voltkart.in.
- **Cancellation Due to Non-Delivery**: If a delayed order cannot be fulfilled, it will be cancelled with a 100% full refund returned to your source account within 5-7 business days.
