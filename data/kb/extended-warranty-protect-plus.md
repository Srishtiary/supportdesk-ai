# VoltKart Protect+ Extended Warranty Plans

VoltKart Protect+ provides extended protection plans designed to safeguard your electronics against unexpected mechanical and electrical failures long after the standard 1-year warranty expires.

## Extended Coverage and Key Benefits

VoltKart Protect+ extends the official manufacturer warranty by an additional 1 or 2 years:
- **100% Cashless Repairs**: All genuine spare parts, diagnostic fees, and authorized technician labor costs are fully covered up to the original invoice value of the item.
- **Genuine Replacement Parts**: Repairs are completed strictly using original OEM components at certified service facilities.
- **No-Lemon Policy**: If the same component fails more than three times during the active Protect+ period, or if the product cannot be repaired within 21 business days, VoltKart will replace the unit or refund the depreciated value.
- **Free Doorstep Pickup and Drop**: Available across all major metro cities and Tier 2 hubs in India.

VoltKart Protect+ kicks in automatically the day after your standard 1-year manufacturer warranty concludes, ensuring continuous, unbroken coverage.

## Purchasing Window and Activation

Customers can add VoltKart Protect+ during or right after purchasing their device:
- **At Checkout**: Select the 1-year or 2-year Protect+ addon directly on the product detail page or shopping cart before completing payment.
- **Post-Purchase Window**: You may purchase Protect+ up to 30 days following product delivery, provided the item is fully functional and inspected.
- **Activation Details**: Your digital certificate of extended warranty is sent via email and registered in your VoltKart account profile within 48 hours of purchase.
- Protect+ plans are transferable if the underlying device is gifted or sold, provided the original invoice and serial number remain intact.

## Filing a Protect+ Service Claim

Filing a claim under your Protect+ plan takes just a few steps:
1. Log in to your VoltKart account and navigate to "My Orders" > "Protect+ Plans".
2. Select your registered device, describe the technical fault, and upload pictures or video demonstrating the issue.
3. Our technical team verifies the claim within 24 hours and arranges a complimentary courier pickup or assigns a home technician for large appliances.
4. If a repair cannot be completed and a refund is approved under our policy, funds are dispatched within 5-7 business days to your original payment method or wallet.
