# Customer Support Channels and Grievance Escalation

VoltKart is committed to providing prompt, attentive support for all your product inquiries, order updates, and technical questions.

## Primary Customer Support Contact Channels

Our dedicated support team is available through multiple communication touchpoints:
- **Toll-Free Phone Support**: Call our customer helpline at 1800-890-VOLT (1800-890-8658). Lines are open seven days a week from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM IST.
- **Email Support**: Write to us anytime at support@voltkart.in. Please mention your 8-digit Order ID (e.g., VK-984210) for expedited resolution.
- **24/7 Live Chat**: Accessible directly from the bottom right corner of the VoltKart website and mobile app for instant assistance with automated bot answers or live agent support.
- **Self-Service Portal**: Initiate returns within the 30-day return window, request order cancellations, or track live courier movements directly through "My Orders".

## Escalation Matrix and Nodal Grievance Redressal

If your concern is not resolved to your satisfaction through front-line support channels, follow our tiered grievance escalation path:
- **Level 1 (Customer Support Specialist)**: Contact via email or phone. First-response time is within 12 hours.
- **Level 2 (Customer Support Manager)**: If unresolved after 48 hours, reply to your open ticket requesting escalation to support-manager@voltkart.in.
- **Level 3 (Grievance Officer)**: In compliance with the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, consumer grievances may be addressed directly to our Grievance Officer:
  - **Officer Name**: Rajesh Nair, Nodal Grievance Officer
  - **Email**: grievances@voltkart.in
  - **Address**: VoltKart Retail Private Limited, Prestige Tech Cloud, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560066, India
  - **Turnaround Time**: Acknowledgment within 24 hours, with full inquiry and resolution within 15 working days.

## Service Level Agreements (SLA) and Response Standards

We maintain transparent performance metrics to ensure our customers receive reliable service:
- **Order Cancellation Inquiries**: Responded to within 2 hours during active business hours.
- **Damaged or Missing Item Reports**: Addressed within 24 hours of reporting (provided reports are submitted within the 48-hour post-delivery window).
- **Return Approvals & Reverse Pickup**: Reverse pickups scheduled within 24 to 48 hours of return authorization.
- **Refund Initiation**: Processed within 24 hours of warehouse inspection, reaching customer accounts within 5-7 business days for digital payments or immediately for store credit.
- **Warranty Coordination**: Assistance for brand repair facilitation provided within 48 hours under our standard 1-year warranty assistance framework.
