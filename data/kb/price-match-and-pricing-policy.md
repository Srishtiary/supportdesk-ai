# Pricing, GST Invoicing, and Price Drop Protection

VoltKart is committed to honest, all-inclusive pricing, transparent commercial terms, and statutory GST compliance for retail and enterprise electronics buyers in India.

## All-Inclusive Pricing and GST Invoicing

All prices displayed on VoltKart are inclusive of applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST):
- **Retail Customers**: Every shipment includes an official GST tax invoice showing base price, CGST, SGST, or IGST breakdowns, and serial numbers of the dispatched items.
- **Business Purchases (B2B)**: Registered businesses can enter a valid 15-digit GSTIN and company legal name during checkout. A compliant GST B2B invoice will be generated, enabling your business to claim Input Tax Credit (ITC).
- **Download Invoices**: Digital copies of your GST invoices are retained permanently in your account and can be downloaded anytime under "My Orders".
- Free standard shipping applies to all orders with total invoice value exceeding Rs 999; orders of Rs 999 or lower carry a flat Rs 49 delivery fee.

## 7-Day Price Drop Protection Guarantee

To give customers confidence when shopping on VoltKart, we provide a 7-day price drop protection window:
- If an item you purchased on VoltKart drops in listed price within 7 calendar days of your order date, you can request a price match.
- The item must be the exact identical brand, model, storage/color configuration, and seller SKU.
- Once verified by our support team, the net difference is credited as VoltKart Store Credit within 24 to 48 hours.
- Price drop protection does not apply to lightning flash sales, clearance blowout events, bank-specific credit card instant discounts, or promotional coupon redemptions.
- Store credit issued under price drop protection is valid for 1 year (365 days).

## Promotional Discounts, Coupons, and Pricing Errors

VoltKart regularly features coupon codes, bank tie-ups, and festive discounts:
- Only one promotional coupon code can be applied per checkout transaction unless explicitly stated otherwise.
- In the rare event of an obvious technical pricing glitch (e.g., a Rs 90,000 laptop listed erroneously for Rs 900), VoltKart reserves the right to cancel the order prior to dispatch, with a 100% refund credited back within 5-7 business days.
- In case of returned items where a tiered coupon or threshold promotion was applied (such as free shipping above Rs 999), your refund will be recalculated based on the retained items.
