# VoltKart Returns and Replacements Policy

VoltKart offers a hassle-free return and replacement policy for all electronic devices and accessories purchased through our platform across India.

## 30-Day Return Window and Eligibility

Customers are eligible to request a return or replacement within 30 days from the date of product delivery. To qualify for a return or replacement, the item must fulfill the following criteria:
- The product must be in its original, unused condition without scratches, dents, or signs of wear.
- All original packaging, user manuals, warranty cards, cable ties, and manufacturer accessories must be included.
- The original brand box, price tags, and the device's International Mobile Equipment Identity (IMEI) or serial number barcode must be intact and legible.
- The original tax invoice provided by VoltKart must be presented during reverse pickup.

Products that show signs of physical tampering, unauthorized software modifications (such as rooting or custom ROM flashing), or accidental damage are not eligible for return or replacement under this policy.

## Step-by-Step Return and Replacement Process

Initiating a return or replacement is simple through your VoltKart customer account:
1. **Submit Request**: Log in to your VoltKart account, navigate to "My Orders", choose the eligible order, and click "Request Return/Replacement".
2. **Select Reason**: Choose the relevant reason from the dropdown menu and upload clear photographs of the product, packaging, and serial number tag.
3. **Doorstep Pickup**: Once your request is verified within 24 to 48 hours, our logistics partner will schedule a free reverse pickup from your registered delivery address.
4. **Inspection and Resolution**: After the item reaches our fulfillment center and passes technical inspection (typically within 24 to 48 hours of receipt), we will process your replacement dispatch or issue a refund within 5-7 business days to your original payment method.

If you choose a replacement and the identical model is out of stock, VoltKart will issue a full refund or store credit according to your preference.

## Non-Returnable Products and Exceptions

Certain categories of products cannot be returned due to hygiene, safety, or digital licensing restrictions:
- **Consumables and Chemicals**: Unsealed thermal pastes, cleaning gels, aerosol spray cleaners, and single-use disposable batteries cannot be returned once opened.
- **Personal Audio Items**: In-ear earphones and true wireless stereo (TWS) earbuds cannot be returned once unsealed for hygienic reasons, unless delivered dead-on-arrival or verified defective within 48 hours.
- **Digital Content**: Software download licenses, anti-virus keys, extended warranty codes, and digital gift cards are non-refundable once emailed or revealed.
- **Customized Items**: Custom-built desktop PCs and engraved accessories cannot be returned unless verified to contain defective hardware.
