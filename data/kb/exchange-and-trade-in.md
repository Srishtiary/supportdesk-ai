# VoltKart Device Exchange and Trade-in Program

VoltKart's Device Exchange program allows you to trade in your old electronics—such as smartphones, tablets, laptops, and smartwatches—for an instant discount on your new purchase.

## Exchange Eligibility and Valuation Process

Trading in your pre-owned device is integrated directly into the checkout process:
- **Eligible Devices**: Used smartphones, laptops, smartwatches, and tablets from approved manufacturers can be traded in.
- **Online Assessment**: Select "Buy with Exchange" on any eligible product page, select your current device's brand and model, and answer diagnostic questions regarding screen condition, body scratches, camera functionality, and battery health.
- **Instant Discount**: An upfront exchange discount value is calculated and deducted immediately from your shopping cart subtotal.
- **Shipping Threshold Calculation**: The cart value after exchange deduction must be at least Rs 999 to qualify for free standard shipping; otherwise, the standard shipping fee of Rs 49 is added.

## Doorstep Verification and Handover Guidelines

When your new device is delivered, the exchange transaction takes place simultaneously at your doorstep:
- **Device Preparation**: Back up your personal files, log out of cloud services (iCloud, Google Account, Mi Account), and perform a full factory reset before the courier executive arrives.
- **Physical Inspection**: The delivery partner will perform a quick 5-minute visual and software check to verify the screen condition, touchscreen responsiveness, camera, and IMEI/serial number against your initial submission.
- **Battery Life**: Ensure your old device has at least 30% battery charge so the technician can power it on and run diagnostic checks.
- **Handover**: Once verified, you hand over the old unit and receive your brand-new item. All new items are protected by VoltKart's standard 30-day return window and 1-year brand warranty.

## Valuation Adjustments and Exchange Cancellations

If your old device condition differs from the initial online description:
- **Revised Valuation**: The delivery agent's diagnostic tool may calculate a revised valuation. You may pay the difference on the spot using UPI or cash to accept delivery.
- **Rejecting the Revised Offer**: If you do not agree with the reassessed valuation, you can cancel the exchange portion. You can either pay the full original price difference to keep the new item, or cancel the order without penalty.
- **Refund Policy for Exchanges**: If you subsequently return the newly purchased product within its 30-day return period, you will receive a refund for the net amount paid within 5-7 business days. Old exchanged devices cannot be returned once handed over and processed.
